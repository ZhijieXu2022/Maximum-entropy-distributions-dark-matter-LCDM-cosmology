# Maximum entropy distributions of dark matter in ΛCDM cosmology

Xu Z., 2023, *Maximum entropy distributions of dark matter in ΛCDM cosmology*, A&A 675, A92, [doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202346429](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346429); arXiv: [2110.03126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03126).

## Major findings

- The maximum entropy distribution of dark matter particle velocity (the X distribution) is derived analytically from the virial theorem and the maximum entropy principle, without assuming a halo mass function.
- The X distribution involves a shape parameter α and a velocity scale v0.
- The shape parameter α reflects the range of the force: the distribution tends to Laplacian as α → 0 (long-range force) and to Gaussian as α → ∞ (short-range force).
- For intermediate α, the distribution has a Gaussian core for v ≪ v0 and exponential wings for v ≫ v0.
- The mean energy of dark matter particles at a given speed v scales as v² for v ≪ v0 (halo core) and linearly with v for v ≫ v0 (halo outskirts).
- The halo mass function emerges from the maximum entropy formulation rather than being an input.
- The predictions agree well with N-body simulations.
